# iDreamMusic Muse Portrait Prompts

Create a unified series of nine oval-framed portraits for the Nine Muses of iDreamMusic. Each portrait should depict a classical marble-statue-like feminine figure with living elegance, holding an iconic symbolic object. The set must feel cohesive, elevated, timeless, mythic, and refined.

Shared style rules for all nine:
- Oval portrait composition
- Classical statue aesthetic with soft lifelike detail
- Mythic editorial fine-art style
- Marble, moonlight, stone, subtle gold accents
- Dark luminous background with individual mood variation by Muse
- No text in image

1. Calliope — Story: scroll and lantern or quill
2. Clio — Roots: branch, old key, or map-like relic
3. Erato — Love: rose, ribbon, dove, or heart-shaped locket
4. Euterpe — Craft: lyre, manuscript, or tuning symbol
5. Melpomene — Blues: weathered guitar, broken chain, or dark rose
6. Polyhymnia — Faith: candle, sacred flame, cross, or prayerful veil
7. Terpsichore — Rhythm: tambourine, drum, or flowing scarf
8. Thalia — Play: bells, playful theatrical mask, or laughing garland
9. Urania — Dream: moon bowl, constellation sphere, or celestial vessel
